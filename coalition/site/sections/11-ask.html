The ask

There are four ways to be in this, and only one of them is code.

Find the row that describes you. That is the ask.

  1. 01

    Nonprofits and service orgs

    Be a founding organization.

    Post your events, your services, and your volunteer needs. You are the first real content in here, and you stop paying to reach people who already asked to hear from you.

  2. 02

    ATU faculty

    Put a capstone team on one of the project briefs.

    Students build on a running platform instead of building from zero, and nothing they make is orphaned in April.

  3. 03

    Community leaders

    Be part of the seed cohort.

    Residency is verified by neighbors vouching for neighbors. That has to start with people the town already trusts.

  4. 04

    Anyone who builds

    Make something.

    The tooling is live. If you have an idea and an afternoon, you now have somewhere to put it.
