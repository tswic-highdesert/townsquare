What we think the answer is

Not a better newsletter. Something the whole community can build on.

Town Square is built in three layers, and each one asks more of the town than the one before it. That order is deliberate. It has to be useful before anyone is asked to participate, and useful and busy before anyone is asked to build.

1

Bring in what already exists

We start by pulling the town's existing signals into one place. Business listings, news sites, event calendars, public notices, podcasts. Everything already out there and already worth having, gathered.

Nobody has to change what they do for this to work.

No organization has to adopt a new platform, learn a new tool, or move their calendar. We come to the information where it already lives. On day one, before a single resident signs up, there is already something worth walking up to.

2

Let the town add what only people can add

Residents add what no scraper will ever find. What is actually going on, what is worth going to, what a place is really like. Reviews, recommendations, notices, corrections, answers.

Then the difference that matters, and it is the whole difference.

We do not sell ads, so we have no reason to hide anything from you.

We have no ad business to protect. If you asked to see it, you see it.

Why an ad feed has to hide things

An ad-funded feed makes money by controlling the slot in front of you, so it has to decide what you do not see in order to sell that space back to the people who made it. That is why the nonprofit you followed on purpose has to pay to reach you. It is not a bug in the system, it is the system.

Why removing ads is not enough

Worth saying plainly, because it is the trap. Ranking purely by engagement recreates the same problem even without ads, since outrage is engaging. Taking away the ad motive does not, on its own, get you a place that treats you honestly.

So the rule is this. Discovery only ever adds to what you asked for. It never takes away.

3

Let anyone build on it

In 2026 a person with an idea and an afternoon can build a real working app. What they cannot do is give it a home, an audience, or any connection to anything else in their town. That is what this is.

A note on the layers

Discovery is not one of them. Finding the thing you did not know to look for is what all three layers are for, not a step in the middle.
