What we think the answer is

Town Square is a place built for the life of this town.

It brings what is already happening into view, gives people a way to add what they know, and makes room for anyone to build what the town needs next.

Open it, and you find a town already in motion.

Nothing here is waiting to be filled in. The day is already there.

Nobody has to change what they do

We start by pulling the town's existing signals into one place. Business listings, news sites, event calendars, public notices, podcasts. Everything that is already out there and already worth having, gathered.

No organization has to adopt a new platform, learn a new tool, or move their calendar. We come to the information where it already lives. On day one, before a single resident signs up, there is already something worth walking up to.

Some things you only know if you live here

Whether the event is actually worth going to. Which mechanic will not run you around. What a place is really like on a Saturday morning. Where to actually park. Who will take care of you and who will not.

None of that is written down anywhere. It lives in people's heads. This is a place to put it.
