the people most affected in defining root causes, and that story is the
requirement, met.

made was attribution and free publishing for Farm Bureau county events rather
than ad placement. A no here costs nothing and tells us something.

## Conversations that opened a door

### 2026-08-07, Madelyn Ginsberg, Arkansas Community Foundation Pope County

Called to resolve which grant cycle applies. Outcome was better than the answer.

**What was settled.** The Pope County affiliate runs the **January cycle**, not
the statewide fall round that closed 2026-08-15. There was no eight-day sprint
to miss. The target is January 2027, with roughly five months to build the
relationship first, which is the stronger position anyway.

**What was opened.** She responded well to the idea and **invited Tal to present
to the Pope County affiliate advisory board whenever he wants**. That board is
the committee of Pope County residents who actually decide the grants. An open
invitation to present to the deciders, months before the cycle opens, is worth
more than the grant.

**The catch, and it is a real one.** Madelyn has **retired**. She is taking phone
calls for the next few months while her successor settles in, and then she is
gone. The invitation came from her personally, and the relationship currently
lives with a person on her way out the door. A successor who has never heard of
Town Square inherits nothing unless someone hands it over.

**Decision, 2026-08-07.** Tal is deliberately tabling the board presentation
until the ATU engagements are further along, so he can walk in with a coalition
and a capstone commitment rather than an idea. That is the right call on
substance. ATU contacts selected capstone partners by mid-September, so a
presentation in October or November still lands well ahead of the January cycle.

**What should not wait.** Two cheap things protect the opening without spending
it early:

1. Send Madelyn something short in writing while she is still answering the
   phone, so the idea is documented with the affiliate rather than living in one
   retiring person's memory.
2. Ask her to introduce the incoming coordinator by name. A warm handoff from
   the outgoing officer is the whole asset here, and it expires when she does.
