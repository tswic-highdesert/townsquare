# Entergy / Arkansas Nuclear One giving contact

Who to call at Entergy and Arkansas Nuclear One about community grants for Town Square in
Russellville. Every contact below is sourced. Nothing here is invented. Confidence levels
are marked so you know what is safe to dial.

## Call this number first

**Brandi Hinkle, Entergy Arkansas contributions coordinator: 501-377-4382.**

She is the person Entergy's own grant pages tell applicants to contact before submitting.
Her actual job is running Entergy Arkansas corporate and foundation giving, so this is the
right desk, not a switchboard. Confidence: high. Source:
https://www.entergy.com/communities/coordinators

Script:

"Hi Brandi, this is Tal Swicegood in Russellville. Entergy's grants page lists you as the
Arkansas contributions coordinator and says to talk with you before applying, so I wanted to
ask whether what we are building fits your Open Grants or Micro Grants criteria. We run Town

**Then call the local one: Omar Clemons, Entergy Arkansas area customer service manager for
Russellville.** He is also the 2026 President of the Russellville Area Chamber of Commerce
board, which means he is deeply wired into local civic life and is the natural warm
introduction. No direct line is published. Easiest route is through the Chamber at
479-968-2530 or the Entergy Russellville office at 305 South Knoxville Avenue. Confidence on
the role: high, he is named on the Chamber's live 2026 board page.

## How Entergy giving actually works

Two rolling programs, plus the Foundation behind them.

**Micro Grants.** Up to $1,000. Funds arts and culture, community improvement or enrichment,
economic development, education and literacy, environment, and healthy families. Applications
accepted year round.
Portal: https://bbgm-apply.yourcausegrants.com/apply/programs/fd5946d8-56eb-4cdc-9363-4a6654bc95df

**Open Grants.** Larger amounts. Funds arts and culture, education and workforce development,
poverty solutions and social services, healthy families, and community improvement. Rolling,
no cycle in Arkansas. Mississippi has an April 1 deadline; Arkansas does not.
Portal: https://bbgm-apply.yourcausegrants.com/apply/programs/a03148a5-cbbb-4e18-9cf1-03e9eb40fec2

**Stated lead time.** Submit at least three months before you need the money, and allow 8 to
12 weeks for review and notification. Plan on roughly a quarter from application to answer.

**Who decides.** Applications go through a corporate portal (YourCause / Blackbaud), but
Entergy's own instruction is to contact the local contributions coordinator with questions
before and after applying, which is what makes Hinkle the gatekeeper for Arkansas. Entergy
Nuclear has its own separate coordinator (Donicia Thompson), and ANO's roughly $100,000 a
year in local River Valley grants appears to be administered at the site level, which points
back to the Site VP's office at ANO. Practically: apply through the Arkansas track with
Hinkle, and work Clemons and ANO for the local River Valley pot in parallel.

**Eligibility, and the one thing to check first.** Grants go only to a 501(c)(3) public
charity or private foundation, a school, hospital, governmental unit, or religious institution
with similar status, a state or local government where the donation is exclusively for public
purposes, a 501(c)(19) veterans organization, or a 501(c)(4) volunteer fire department. If
Town Square is not itself a 501(c)(3), you need a fiscal sponsor or a nonprofit or municipal
partner as the applicant of record. Ask Hinkle about this on the first call, because it
determines whether there is an application at all. Explicit exclusions include gala events,
fundraising meals, and program advertisements.

**Scale, for calibration.** Entergy Arkansas awarded more than $2.1 million to over 420
nonprofit partners in 2025, with individual grants ranging roughly $500 to $50,000. In 2024
it was about $1.8 to $2 million across 240 partners, averaging near $5,000. So a realistic
first ask is small, four to five figures, not a transformational check.

  route you to giving, though it is the only published number that reaches the Russellville
  office indirectly.
- **479-968-7171 / 800-422-6630.** This is the Arkansas Department of Health Nuclear Planning
  and Response Program, an emergency planning line. It is a state agency, not Entergy, and it
  has nothing to do with grants.
- **479-858-7826** ANO Regulatory Assurance, and **479-858-5487** the environmental permit
  contact. Both are NRC and regulatory desks. Do not use them.
- **Entergy's newsroom media line.** Brandi Hinkle is sometimes quoted as an Entergy Arkansas
  spokesperson in news stories, but her actual role is CSR and giving. Call her directly at
  501-377-4382 and open with grants, not with a media framing, so you land on the right side
  of her job.
